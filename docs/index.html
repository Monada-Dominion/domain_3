🌐 The Meta-Game Architecture

🎮 Alternate Reality & Meta Space

Mystical Journey is an alternate reality game (ARG). It is not centralized within a single screen or Playstation—the game field spans digital space, physical cities, and real-world locations. Players meet game facilitators, draw cards, receive coordinates, and decode secret messages using physical surroundings.

🏛️ Game Master (GM) Facilitators

In different cities, facilitators keep the physical card set, assign coordinates, and guide players through tabletop or real-world exploration.

🏙️ City & Globe as Game Field

Entire cities can be turned into 12 radial sectors. Players choose areas, travel to assigned coordinates, and decode secret oracle messages.

🎨 Dedicated Spaces & AR/VR

As community participation grows, dedicated installations, graffiti rooms, and AR/VR metaverse creations enrich the game universe.
